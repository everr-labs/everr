Pick the channel that fits what you need, and we will get back to you there.

## Email

Write to [hello@everr.dev](mailto:hello@everr.dev) for anything that does not fit a public channel: account and billing questions, partnerships, press, or privacy requests.

## Support and questions

Join the [Everr Discord community](https://discord.gg/hd6yYDjAuw) for help with setup, instrumentation, queries, alerts, or anything else about using Everr. It is the fastest way to reach the team, and other users often share what worked for them. Feature requests and feedback are welcome there too.

Before you ask, check the [documentation](/docs): it covers installation, OpenTelemetry setup, production telemetry, dashboards, alerts, runbooks, and the full CLI reference.

## Sales, higher volumes, and dedicated instances

If you need more capacity than the published plans offer, a dedicated instance, or a custom agreement, [book a call with the team](https://calendar.app.google/kgnoxBsk4QyoLywG8). The [pricing page](/pricing) lists the current plans and what each includes.

## Bug reports and contributions

Everr is open source. Report bugs in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/everr-labs/everr/issues) and follow development in the [repository](https://github.com/everr-labs/everr). Pull requests are welcome; read the [contributing guide](https://github.com/everr-labs/everr/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) first.
