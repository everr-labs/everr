A destination independently selected by Customer is not automatically our subprocessor. Providers we engage to perform our processing obligations remain our responsibility.

Selected hosting is described in Schedule 3. EU storage does not guarantee all operations or access stay in the EU. Restricted international transfers require a valid mechanism and any necessary assessment and supplementary measures under GDPR Arts. 44 to 49 and other applicable law. Relevant location and safeguard information is available on reasonable request. We will put the required safeguards in place before a restricted transfer occurs. This DPA does not itself execute Standard Contractual Clauses or another transfer instrument.

Each party ordinarily bears its review costs. Reasonable charges for substantial additional assistance may be agreed in advance, except where caused by our breach or law requires otherwise. Fee disputes must not block legally required assistance or access.

## Return and deletion

Customer instructs us to delete Customer Personal Data at the end of processing unless Customer requests return instead through [hello@everr.dev](mailto:hello@everr.dev). When account or organization closure ends Customer's service, we will complete deletion of Customer Personal Data from active systems through our automated deletion process without undue delay and within 30 calendar days of closure, without requiring a separate deletion request, subject to a pending return request, legally required retention, and any shorter applicable legal deadline. We will preserve data needed to complete a pending return request until delivery, then delete remaining copies as described below. Backup and residual provider copies follow the expiry, permanent-deletion, and protection provisions below.

Please arrange return before access ends where possible. Ending service access does not remove Customer's end-of-processing choice of return or deletion under GDPR Art. 28(3)(g). Requests after access ends are handled through the contact above; no additional self-service access period is promised.

We will deliver requested data securely in JSON or CSV, as appropriate to the data, with timestamps, identifiers, and associated attributes preserved. Return will be completed without undue delay and within 30 calendar days of the request. Following return, we will delete remaining copies from active systems within 30 calendar days of delivery. If Customer chooses deletion instead, we will complete deletion from active systems without undue delay and within 30 calendar days of the request. Any shorter applicable legal deadline takes precedence.

We also carry out lawful deletion instructions and assist with erasure requests during service, without undue delay and within 30 calendar days of the request or any shorter applicable legal deadline. Requests must identify the organization and relevant data so authorization can be checked. Normal retention expiry does not replace required deletion. We will apply these duties to Customer Personal Data in our control, including support copies and temporary exports, and require our subprocessors to carry out the corresponding return and deletion obligations. On request, we will confirm completion in writing and identify any copies awaiting backup expiry or retained by law, with their applicable expiry periods or retention criteria.

We remove diagnostic support messages and attachments containing Customer Personal Data from active support mailboxes, including trash, once the relevant case is resolved and the material is no longer needed for support, subject to pending return requests and legally required retention. The deletion duties above also apply to downloaded, forwarded, or exported copies under our control. Permanent deletion of residual Google Workspace email copies from Google's systems follows its Cloud Data Processing Addendum, which allows up to 180 days after the applicable irrecoverable deletion instruction, subject to legally required storage. This provider deletion period does not extend our deadline for removing data from active support mailboxes.

Backup copies of deleted data will expire within seven days after deletion from active systems for Scaleway Postgres. ClickHouse telemetry backups have a one-day retention period. Permanent deletion of ClickHouse backup copies, caches, and residual references to deleted telemetry may take up to 15 calendar days after deletion from active systems. Copies awaiting expiry or permanent deletion must remain protected, isolated from ordinary use, and unused for new purposes. Relevant deletions must be reapplied before restored data returns to ordinary processing. Legally required retention must be limited to necessary data, purpose, and duration, with the requirement explained to Customer where lawful. Protection under this DPA continues until all retained copies are deleted.

## Schedule 2: Security measures

Measures appropriate to risk will include:

- Confidential, authorized access limited to necessary tasks, administrator multi-factor authentication, and access review/removal.
- Organization permissions and logical separation across storage, queries, exports, and support.
- Appropriate transport and storage encryption, with protected credentials and keys.
- Collection controls and procedures for locating data and handling rights, return, and deletion.
- Controlled backups, recovery procedures and tests, and reapplication of deletions after restoration.
- Risk-based vulnerability handling, maintenance, change review, and periodic assessment of safeguards.
- Proportionate security records, incident detection/response, and customer notification.
- Supplier review and documented assistance with rights, assessments, and audits.

Operational logs follow the stated one-year policy, subject to necessary purpose and any applicable deletion duty. This schedule claims no certification or completed audit.
