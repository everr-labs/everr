## Scope and instructions

This DPA supplements the [Terms](/terms) or a separate service agreement between Customer and **Giordano Ricci**, at **Rua das Meloas 10C, 2805-235 Almada, Portugal** ("Everr"). Our privacy contact is [hello@everr.dev](mailto:hello@everr.dev); Customer's contact is its designated organization administrator or another contact notified in writing. **Version and effective date: [TO BE SET].**

It covers personal data in Customer Data processed on Customer's behalf ("Customer Personal Data") under applicable data-protection law, including EU and UK GDPR where applicable. Customer is controller or an authorized processor appointing Everr as subprocessor. Everr is processor or subprocessor according to the actual activity. Our own account, billing, and other controller processing is described in the [privacy notice](/privacy); customer telemetry shared for support remains within this DPA.

We process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions to provide and secure the agreed service. Instructions comprise the service agreement, this DPA, authorized configuration and use, and lawful written directions from an authorized contact. Transfers to customer-selected destinations must also be lawful. Instructions outside the agreed scope require prior assessment and agreement.

We will notify Customer before legally compelled processing outside instructions unless law prohibits notice, and promptly flag instructions we consider unlawful. Affected processing may be suspended while resolving an unlawful instruction. This does not excuse ignoring lawful instructions or legal duties.

Customer is responsible for collection purposes, legal bases, required notices and consents, configuration, submission rights, and authority from any upstream controller. Restricted Data requires prior written agreement and safeguards under the Terms. This DPA grants no permission for our own advertising, sale, or general-purpose model training on Customer Personal Data. Neither party's responsibilities remove the other's legal duties.

## Security and confidentiality

We will apply safeguards appropriate to the processing and risks, including Schedule 2, and require confidentiality from authorized personnel. Access must be limited to necessary tasks. Protection and confidentiality continue while data is held. Security updates must not materially reduce the agreed protection.

Customer secures its systems, credentials, collection settings, and chosen destinations. Everr remains responsible for its own processing security under GDPR Arts. 28 and 32 where applicable.

## Subprocessors and transfers

Customer generally authorizes the subprocessors in the completed Schedule 3 for their listed functions. We will bind them to written protections required for their delegated processing and remain responsible for their performance as required by GDPR Art. 28(4).

We will email the designated contact at least 30 calendar days before adding or replacing a subprocessor, identifying its function and locations. Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within 14 calendar days. We will seek a reasonable alternative or mitigation and will not begin disputed processing while the objection remains unresolved. If no lawful, commercially reasonable solution is available, affected processing will not proceed and either party may end the affected service, subject to the Terms and mandatory remedies.

A destination independently selected by Customer is not automatically our subprocessor. Providers we engage to perform our processing obligations remain our responsibility.

Selected hosting is described in Schedule 3. EU storage does not guarantee all operations or access stay in the EU. Restricted international transfers require a valid mechanism and any necessary assessment and supplementary measures under GDPR Arts. 44 to 49 and other applicable law. Relevant location and safeguard information is available on reasonable request. Applicable contracting entities, transfers, and safeguards must be confirmed before this draft takes effect; it does not execute transfer clauses.

## Rights, assessments, and incidents

Taking account of the processing and information available, we will provide appropriate assistance with individual rights, security, breach notifications, impact assessments, and prior consultations under GDPR Art. 28(3)(e)-(f). Customer remains responsible for its own decisions and filings. We will cooperate with authorities as legally required.

We will promptly forward requests about Customer Personal Data that identify Customer and reach us directly. We will respond on Customer's behalf only when authorized or legally required. Both parties must cooperate promptly to meet legal deadlines.

We will notify the designated contact without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting Customer Personal Data. As available, notice will describe affected data and individuals, approximate numbers, likely consequences, response measures, and a contact. Initial notice may be followed by updates. We will take reasonable containment and remediation steps, preserve relevant evidence, and assist with required notifications. Notification does not by itself admit liability or reduce these duties. Customer must keep its contact details current.

## Information and audits

We will supply information needed to demonstrate compliance and permit and contribute to legally required audits, including inspections (GDPR Art. 28(3)(h)). Initial review may use written information and existing independent reports; no certification is claimed here.

Routine audits require reasonable coordination, qualified confidential auditors, and protection of other customers and security information. Ordinarily one routine audit per twelve months is sufficient, with additional review for legal or regulatory requirements, relevant incidents, or reasonable material-compliance concerns. Arrangements must not prevent necessary audits. Unrelated data may be redacted or inspected securely.

Each party ordinarily bears its review costs. Reasonable charges for substantial additional assistance may be agreed in advance, except where caused by our breach or law requires otherwise. Fee disputes must not block legally required assistance or access.

## Return and deletion

At the end of processing, Customer chooses return or deletion of Customer Personal Data, with remaining copies deleted unless law requires retention. We also carry out lawful deletion instructions and assist with erasure requests during service. Requests must identify the organization and relevant data so authorization can be checked.

Arrange return before access ends. Return will use an appropriate commonly used machine-readable format; deletion confirmation is available on request. No additional self-service access period is promised.

**Early telemetry deletion and the return procedure are not yet verified. This DPA remains a draft until those capabilities and timing are confirmed. Normal expiry, which can take one year, does not replace required deletion.**

Backup expiry is seven days for Scaleway Postgres and one day for ClickHouse telemetry. Copies awaiting expiry must remain protected, isolated from ordinary use, and unused for new purposes. Relevant deletions must be reapplied before restored data returns to ordinary processing. Legally required retention must be limited to necessary data, purpose, and duration, with the requirement explained to Customer where lawful. These duties implement the intended return/deletion arrangements under GDPR Art. 28(3)(g).

## Liability and priority

The service agreement's liability rules apply to contractual DPA claims, sharing one aggregate limit across both documents, with the same paid-business, free-business, and exception provisions. This DPA adds no indemnity or separate uncapped category for confidentiality, security, or data-processing claims.

This does not limit performance duties, individuals' statutory rights, regulatory powers, or liability that cannot lawfully be limited. Neither party separately undertakes to reimburse regulatory fines imposed on the other; directly imposed legal liability remains unaffected.

This DPA continues while covered data is held, including during return, deletion, or lawful retention. It controls processing conflicts with the service agreement, subject to mandatory law and mandatory transfer clauses. Other commercial matters follow the service agreement.

## Schedule 2: Security measures

**Proposed commitments pending verification before acceptance.**

Measures appropriate to risk will include:

- Confidential, authorized access limited to necessary tasks, administrator multi-factor authentication, and access review/removal.
- Organization permissions and logical separation across storage, queries, exports, and support.
- Appropriate transport and storage encryption, with protected credentials and keys.
- Collection controls and procedures for locating data and handling rights, return, and deletion.
- Controlled backups, recovery procedures and tests, and reapplication of deletions after restoration.
- Risk-based vulnerability handling, maintenance, change review, and periodic assessment of safeguards.
- Proportionate security records, incident detection/response, and customer notification.
- Supplier review and documented assistance with rights, assessments, and audits.

Operational logs follow the stated one-year policy, subject to necessary purpose and any applicable deletion duty. Implementation details remain subject to verification; this schedule claims no certification or completed audit.
