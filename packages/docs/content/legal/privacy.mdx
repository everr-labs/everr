## Information we collect

We collect or receive the following information when you visit our website, use Everr, or contact us:

- **Account and organization information:** your name, email address, profile image, authentication details, account dates, organization name, memberships, roles, invitations, and API key metadata. You, your organization, or an external sign-in provider supply this information.
- **Billing and customer records:** billing contacts, plan and subscription details, transaction and checkout references, and customer relationship records. Polar and its payment providers handle payment details entered at checkout.
- **Usage and technical information:** pages and links visited, referring pages, browser and device information, interactions, performance, errors, network request metadata, and IP addresses. We receive this information from browsers and our infrastructure.
- **Communications:** contact details, messages, attachments, and diagnostic information you send to us.
- **Integration information:** information from services you or your organization connect, such as GitHub repository and workflow metadata, usernames, and contributor information.

We need account details to create and secure an account, and billing information for a paid subscription. Without them, we may be unable to provide the relevant service. Additional information in support requests is optional. Please do not send passwords, access tokens, or unrelated personal data.

## Analytics and browser storage

We use PostHog Cloud EU and Everr browser monitoring to understand website use and identify performance problems and errors. The information received can include URLs, browser information, and IP addresses.

PostHog is configured without analytics cookies or browser storage. On our website, Everr browser monitoring keeps visitor and session identifiers in memory rather than persistent browser storage. Events already received by our servers are not deleted when those identifiers expire.

The Everr application uses authentication cookies and stores your analytics preference. If you agree, browser monitoring can store visitor and session identifiers in local storage. If you decline or withdraw that choice, it uses in-memory identifiers instead; browser telemetry continues to be sent. You can change your choice under **Privacy preferences** in the user menu. Blocking necessary cookies may prevent sign-in or account features from working.

## Customer telemetry

Customers decide what information their applications send to Everr. Logs, traces, metrics, and attributes may contain personal data, such as identifiers, IP addresses, URLs, and information in application logs or errors. We store and query customer telemetry using ClickHouse Cloud on AWS infrastructure in the EU.

If your request concerns telemetry submitted by a customer, please contact the organization operating that application first. You can also contact us for help routing your request.

A local collector does not by itself send locally stored telemetry to Everr Cloud. Sending to a cloud endpoint, enabling cloud integrations, or sharing diagnostics involves a separate transfer.

## International transfers

We host the application, database backups, and customer telemetry in the EU. Some providers operate internationally, so support and other operational processing may take place outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, applicable law requires an appropriate transfer mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or Standard Contractual Clauses with any necessary supplementary measures. Contact us for information about processing locations and safeguards.

For PostHog analytics, our signed Data Processing Agreement incorporates the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for controller-to-processor transfers. Scaleway's Data Processing Agreement provides for EU hosting by default and Standard Contractual Clauses with technical and organizational safeguards for relevant overseas transfers, covering both controller-to-processor and processor-to-processor transfers.

ClickHouse's Data Processing Addendum incorporates Standard Contractual Clauses for relevant restricted transfers, including processor-to-processor clauses where we process customer telemetry on a customer's behalf. Brevo's Data Processing Agreement provides for Standard Contractual Clauses and supplementary measures for relevant international processing, including overseas group support and maintenance.

For billing information processed on our behalf, [Polar's Data Processing Addendum](https://polar.sh/legal/data-processing-addendum) incorporates controller-to-processor Standard Contractual Clauses for relevant transfers outside the EEA. Polar also processes transaction and compliance information for its own purposes under its [Privacy Policy](https://polar.sh/legal/privacy-policy).

To request a copy of applicable contractual transfer safeguards, email [hello@everr.dev](mailto:hello@everr.dev). Copies may be redacted to protect confidential information while preserving the information needed to understand the safeguards.

## How long we keep information

We determine retention based on the purpose of the information, the duration of the customer relationship, the time needed to resolve requests, applicable recordkeeping obligations, and the need to establish or defend legal claims.

Customer telemetry is scheduled to expire according to the [plan's retention window](/docs/reference/retention): 14 days for Hobby and 365 days for Pro, based on the plan when the data is received. Deletion may take additional time. When account or organization closure ends the customer's service, we will remove its telemetry through our automated deletion process under the [Terms](/terms) and [DPA](/dpa). Deletion may take additional time after closure. Pending return requests, legally required retention, and backup expiry follow those provisions.

ClickHouse telemetry backups have a one-day retention period. Permanent deletion of backup copies, caches, and residual references to deleted telemetry may take up to 15 calendar days after deletion from active systems. These copies remain protected and isolated from ordinary use while awaiting permanent deletion.

Account information is kept while the account is active; you can request deletion where applicable. Organization records, billing records, and information retained for legal obligations or claims may be handled separately. Operational logs are kept for one year. Deleted information may remain in backups until those backups expire.

Depending on applicable law and circumstances, you can request access to, correction or deletion of, restriction of, or portability of your personal data. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and exercise protections concerning solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

To exercise your rights, email [hello@everr.dev](mailto:hello@everr.dev). We may request information reasonably needed to verify your identity and locate the data. We normally respond to GDPR requests within one month and explain any permitted extension within that month.

You can complain to a data protection authority where you live or work, or where an infringement occurred. See the [EEA supervisory authorities](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en).
