## Analytics and browser storage

Our website uses PostHog Cloud EU and Everr browser monitoring for page views, interactions, performance, errors, and network request metadata.

PostHog is configured in cookieless mode. This avoids its analytics cookies and browser storage, but requests can still include URLs, browser information, and IP addresses. On the website, Everr's browser monitoring keeps visitor and session identifiers in memory rather than persistent browser storage. Ending an identifier's lifetime does not delete events already received by our servers.

The application uses authentication cookies. Blocking necessary cookies may prevent sign-in or account features from working. It also stores your analytics choice in the `everr.consent` cookie for one year. If you agree, Everr browser monitoring can store visitor and session identifiers in local storage; otherwise, it keeps them in memory. You can change this choice through **Privacy preferences** in the user menu. Declining or withdrawing the choice switches to memory-based identifiers; it does not stop browser telemetry or delete events already received.

Live analytics settings, other storage durations, and the legal basis and consent or exemption position for the actual processing remain under review.

## Customer telemetry

Customers decide what their applications send to Everr. Logs, traces, metrics, and attributes can contain identifiers, IP addresses, URLs, and personal data in application logs or errors. We store and query this telemetry through ClickHouse Cloud on AWS infrastructure in the EU.

For requests about a customer's telemetry, contact the organization operating that application first. You can also email us for help routing a request without disclosing another customer's data.

A local collector does not, by itself, send locally stored telemetry to Everr Cloud. Sending to a cloud endpoint, enabling cloud integrations, or sharing diagnostics involves a separate transfer.

## Your rights

Depending on applicable law and circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, and portability of your personal data; object to processing based on legitimate interests; and exercise protections concerning solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects (GDPR Arts. 15 to 22). Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing (Art. 7(3)).

Email [hello@everr.dev](mailto:hello@everr.dev). We may request information reasonably needed to verify your identity and locate the data. We normally respond to GDPR requests within one month and explain any permitted extension within that month (Art. 12(3)).

You can complain to the authority where you live or work, or where an infringement occurred (Art. 77). See the [EEA supervisory authorities](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en).
