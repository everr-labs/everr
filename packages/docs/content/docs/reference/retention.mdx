---
title: Data Retention
description: How long Everr keeps your traces, logs, and metrics, and how retention behaves when your plan changes.
---

Everr stores your telemetry for a fixed window based on your plan. Once data falls outside that window it is removed automatically and cannot be recovered.

## How long we keep your data

| Signal | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Traces | 14 days | 30 days |
| Logs | 14 days | 30 days |
| Metrics | 14 days | 13 months |

Each signal type is governed by its own retention window. On the Pro plan, metrics stick around for 13 months, long enough to compare current behavior against the same week or month last year. Traces and logs roll off at 30 days, where the per-event volume is much higher and the long-tail value is lower.

## When retention changes take effect

Retention is decided when data is ingested. Every event is stored with the retention window of your plan at that moment, and it is removed when that window ends. A plan change reaches the ingestion pipeline within about a minute, and from then on new data carries the new window. There is no separate migration step.

Data ingested before the change keeps the window it was stored with:

- **Upgrading.** Data ingested after the upgrade is kept for the longer window. Data ingested before it still expires on the previous plan's schedule. On the Free plan that is at most the last 14 days.
- **Downgrading.** Data ingested before the downgrade stays until its original window ends, up to 30 days (13 months for metrics). Data ingested after the downgrade follows the shorter window. Nothing is removed early.

We chose to stamp retention at ingestion (rather than re-evaluate all of your data on every plan change) because it lets us expire data by dropping whole storage segments at once. That keeps the system predictable under load and keeps deletion independent of how many tenants share a cluster. Existing data is never rewritten, so a plan change cannot cause a spike in storage work that slows down queries.

## How we handle failures

Because retention is fixed at ingestion, it does not depend on background processes finding the right window later. The one moving part is the plan lookup during ingestion:

- Retention is looked up from your plan when the collector authenticates your API key, and cached for 30 seconds.
- After a plan change, data ingested before the pipeline picks it up carries the previous plan's window.

If the plan lookup itself is unavailable, ingestion stops rather than storing data with a wrong or missing window. Data waits in the collector and is delivered once the lookup is back. An internal failure can delay ingestion, but it cannot cause data to be deleted earlier than the window it was stored with.

## Custom retention

Custom per-tenant retention is not currently exposed. If you have a compliance, audit, or contractual requirement that needs a non-standard window, [get in touch](mailto:hello@everr.dev) and we'll work something out.
