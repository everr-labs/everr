---
title: Data Retention
description: How long Everr keeps your traces, logs, and metrics, and how retention behaves when your plan changes.
---

Everr keeps your telemetry for a fixed window based on your plan. Once data falls outside that window it is removed automatically and cannot be recovered.

## How long we keep your data

| Signal | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Traces | 14 days | 1 year |
| Logs | 14 days | 1 year |
| Metrics | 14 days | 1 year |

One window per plan, the same for traces, logs and metrics. No signal rolls off before the others.

On the Pro plan, data can stay available for up to a month after its year ends before it is removed.

## When your plan changes

Each event is kept for the window of your plan **at the time we receive it**. Retention is not retroactive, so a plan change only affects data that arrives after it. A new window takes effect within about a minute.

- **Upgrading.** Data that arrives after the upgrade is kept for the longer window. Data you already have still expires on the old schedule, which on the Free plan is at most the last 14 days.
- **Downgrading.** Data you already have keeps its original window, up to a year. Data that arrives after the downgrade follows the shorter window. Nothing is deleted early.

We never delete your data before the window it was stored with, even when something goes wrong on our side.

## Custom retention

Custom retention is not currently available. If you have a compliance, audit, or contractual requirement that needs a different window, [get in touch](mailto:hello@everr.dev) and we'll work something out.
