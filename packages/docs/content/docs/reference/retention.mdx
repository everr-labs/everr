---
title: Data Retention
description: How long Everr keeps your traces, logs, and metrics, and how retention behaves when your plan changes.
---

Everr schedules telemetry to expire based on the plan in effect when it is received. Expiry is based on the event's calendar date, and automatic removal may take additional time. Required deletion or end-of-service handling may result in earlier deletion.

## How long we keep your data

| Signal  | Hobby   | Pro    |
| ------- | ------- | ------ |
| Traces  | 14 days | 1 year |
| Logs    | 14 days | 1 year |
| Metrics | 14 days | 1 year |

One window per plan, the same for traces, logs and metrics. No signal rolls off before the others.

## When your plan changes

Each event is assigned the window of your plan **at the time we receive it**. Retention is not retroactive, so a plan change only affects data that arrives after it. A new window takes effect within about a minute.

- **Upgrading.** Data that arrives after the upgrade is assigned the longer window. Data you already have remains scheduled to expire under the old window.
- **Downgrading.** Data you already have retains its original window, while data that arrives after the downgrade follows the shorter window.

## Custom retention

Custom retention is not currently available. If you have a compliance, audit, or contractual requirement that needs a different window, [get in touch](mailto:hello@everr.dev) and we'll work something out.
