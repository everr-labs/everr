Most observability tools are complex, built for organizations that can afford dedicated telemetry engineers and the time to wire everything up.

At Everr, our mission is to make observability a baseline for software at every level and deliver value from day one. Explore how your software works at runtime and gain insights into your system—without spending days on setup or building a team to manage it.

We’re pursuing our vision of an opinionated yet versatile observability platform that guides you from your first steps in instrumentation through issue investigations, connecting metrics, signals, and logs.

## Our pillars


- **Open**: We love open standards and avoid vendor lock-in. We push [OpenTelemetry](/docs/concepts/whats-opentelemetry) to its limits, releasing additional open-source features when needed.
- **AI-native era**: our skills are specialized capabilities that help AI agents handle observability tasks, such as instrumenting code, querying metrics, and investigating bugs.
- **Local telemetry**: Everr can run a local collector and browser UI, allowing you and your coding assistant to validate ongoing development using telemetry before pushing to production.
- **Configured as code.** Dashboards, alerts, and runbooks are files in your repo, queried in SQL. See [Observability as code](/docs/concepts/observability-as-code).
