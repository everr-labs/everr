Add session recording on the `@everr/otel-web/recording` subpath. `recording()` keeps the page in memory with rrweb, aiming for the last 5 minutes and 10 MB, loaded as a separate chunk for sampled Sessions only. Nothing is sent until a Clip is emitted, automatically on an error, a 5xx response, or a request that gets no response, or with `recorder.emitClip()` and `recorder.startClip()`. Text, inputs, media, and sensitive attributes are masked by default. Recordings leave as `everr.browser.recording.segment` logs and add nothing to traces: each Segment says that the Stream is complete from the start of its run of overlapping Clips through the time the Segment was sent, and the first Segment of a Clip carries its id and trigger. A Clip always ends with a Segment, but a page unload sends nothing, so the last seconds before it can be missing. `clearIdentity()` ends the recording Stream and starts a new one, so no recording joins the ids from before the call to the ids after it.
