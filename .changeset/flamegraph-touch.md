The profiles and trace pages now work on phones and tablets.

- Flamegraphs follow touch: swipe up and down to scroll, drag sideways to pan, pinch to zoom, and tap a frame to see its details and actions in a drawer.
- Clicking a profile flamegraph frame highlights its function instead of zooming in. Zooming to a frame moved to the right-click menu.
- On narrow screens the profiles page shows the flamegraph, the function table and the timeline as tabs. Function details open in a drawer, and you can drag across the timeline chart to pick a time range or a comparison baseline.
- On narrow screens the trace page shows the waterfall and the flamegraph as tabs, opens span details in a drawer, keeps the span filters and display options behind one button, and drops the repeated service name from waterfall rows so operation names have room.
- Resize handles are easier to grab with a finger.
- Buttons and menus are finger-sized on touch screens, including the Filters button on every Explore page.
- The Compare and Version menus close once you pick an option.
