Flame graphs on the profiles and trace pages now work on phones and tablets. Swipe up and down to scroll through the frames, drag sideways to pan, pinch to zoom, tap a frame to select it and see its details, and long-press a frame for its menu. On narrow screens the profiles page scrolls as a whole, so the flame graph and the function table each get a usable height, and the table keeps room for function names.
