The local explorer opened by `everr local start` has a **Metrics** page next to Logs, Traces and Errors. It lists the metrics your app sends to the local collector and charts them the same way as Everr: gauges, counters, UpDownCounters, histograms and exponential histograms (with a heatmap), and summaries, grouped and filtered by attributes. Exemplars on the chart open their trace or its logs. The Service and Environment menus on every local Explore page now include services that send only metrics.
