## Findings (reported, not fixed)
1. Minified bundles (esbuild --minify, rolldown minify) give every built-in instrumentation the name "" (sample-names.txt), so sampled() hashes the same key for recording, errors, and others: their decisions are identical per session. Unminified esbuild renames to recording2/recording3, which changes the key. The code comment relies on the function name.
2. A lost piece of the full snapshot leaves the Clip without a full snapshot; following the spec ("skip it"), the player shows a blank page for the whole Clip (lost-snap).
3. The window clamp kept a start 309.1 s before the call (more than 5 min): trim keeps the newest generation at or before now-5min. By design per the gen[1] rule; noted.
